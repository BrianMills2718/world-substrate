## Why

Brian, 2026-11-16: "it doesnt look good but maybe the overlay just doesnt work", then "can you show me A and B. like have it toggalable maybe?" The whole network drawn over the map is crowded, so the viewer lets him switch between three ways of showing it and judge.

## What Changes

- The graph overlay gets a "Graph style" choice: this round only; whole network on the map; whole network on the map with links shown when a box is clicked; whole network beside the map in columns, where clicking a box lights its links and rings it on the map.
- `?graph=round|map|hover|beside` opens the graph in that style.
