An AI judge that rules only on what no rule covers, fed by everything the engine works out

Status (2026-11-09): complete draft. You approved its direction on 2026-11-09 ("i approve", after Decided 5). Nothing is built yet, and nothing on this page waits on you.

Why. You said, 2026-11-09: "an ai adjudicator doesnt really scale and that we need to facts to have implications and blockers. like 50 things could propagate down a causal chain 100 links deep taht the llm would not be able to understand so we need that causal chain to automaticlaly rpogate and to deliver the resutls of that propagation to he ai judge along with blockers." So the engine does the bookkeeping, however long the chain; the judge only fills gaps, and each ruling becomes a rule everyone is then held to. Other people's choices stay theirs.

1. The examples: follow one attempt

Blue, solid: in the World Builder now Orange, dashed: added by this plan (planned, not built) Grey: what happens today

The port. "A small port: Rosa, the customs officer, signs inspection releases. Vik, a trader, has 40 coins and a crate he has not declared. The exit gate is a barcode scanner that opens for any valid release code. Beyond the gate, a 100-step chain runs: the crate reaches the warehouse, the warehouse fills, the dispatcher sends a truck..." Pick a case; the steps it passes through light up on the left.

1. A person tries somethinghave
A Concordia resident says what it tries in its own words; the interpreter reads it against the installed moves.
↓
2. Does a rule cover it?have
If an installed move fits, the rulebook decides, as today. Today an attempt nothing fits changes nothing.
↓
3. The engine works out every knock-on and blockernew, J0 and J1
Knock-on chains run to the end in one moment (the 32-reaction cap goes; each process and thing still fires once per moment, so loops stop). Every effect and blocker goes into one consequence report.
↓
4. Someone else's choice goes to themnew, J2 (today only plain requests, #326)
If the attempt needs another person to act (accept, sign, refuse), that person's own resident decides. No judge, no rule.
↓
5. The judge rules on the gapnew, J2
Concordia's own game master, given the attempt and the consequence report, rules only on what the world makes possible (can coins change hands here?). It never overrides a blocker and never writes an outcome.
↓
6. The ruling becomes a rulenew, J3
The ruling is written as a rule, passes the World Builder's existing rules check, and is installed at once (default) or held for your Approve button, per the world's setting.
↓
7. From then on the rulebook decides ithave, with the new rule
The same attempt by anyone is decided by the rule, with no judge call; the engine propagates its effects.

Constraints, decisions and norms

You closed this with "i liek the ostrom stuff" (2026-11-09), after: "there is a ddifference between as igned release clearing the cargo as a norm and like some programmatic enry with abar code or whateve.r ... a password managed comptuer where only 1 person was supped to use it with ehir password but if they could be social engineered for exampel to get a password then someone else woul eb abel to sue it."

KindWhat it isPortPassword computerWho owns it
Constraint (what the mechanism checks)What the world makes possible or impossible. Nobody can break it.The barcode gate opens for any valid code; you cannot hand over coins you do not haveThe computer logs in whoever types the right passwordThe engine
Credential holder (a fact)Who holds the code or password. It can change hands: told, overheard, copied, forged, phished.Vik holds a signed releaseAlice knows the password; Mallory can learn it by phishingThe world's state
DecisionWhat a person chooses among what the constraints allow.Vik offers 40 coins; Rosa accepts or refusesAlice answers the phishing message or notThat person's resident
Norm (society's rule)What people expect or forbid. It can be broken; it has consequences only when someone notices and acts. Never a declared law (the engine would make it unbreakable).Bribery is forbidden; only signed cargo should passOnly Alice should log inPeople who notice and act

A gate a guard opens is not a mechanism: it opens when the guard decides to. Cybernetic Influence v3 already kept identity proof, permission, availability and outcome apart, but did not record who holds a credential and left social engineering out of scope; this plan adds both.
