## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Open a completed example from the chat

The chat SHALL offer a completed coordination example with its recorded support counts, matched participant statements and original simulator provenance.

Source: Brian, 2026-11-27, active unified UI goal and docs/goals/unified-world-ux.md.

#### Scenario: Inspect a completed case

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the completed example from the composer and selects its finding
- **THEN** the page shows the recorded statements behind the finding without requiring a new simulation

### Requirement: Inspect a native round from the simple result

The Simple run view SHALL let a visitor open recorded evidence for the selected round in one action.

Source: Brian, 2026-11-27, active unified UI goal and docs/goals/unified-world-ux.md.

#### Scenario: Inspect a round

- **WHEN** a visitor selects the evidence for a round in a native result
- **THEN** the view shows records for the same run and round and keeps supported Advanced controls reachable
